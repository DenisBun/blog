This is the first example post on my new website. It is built with Astro and shaped like an open field notebook: quiet, direct, and made for reading.

## Why this look?

Typography, spacing, and a little paper texture are enough to give writing a sense of place. I wanted the site to feel edited rather than engineered, without giving up modern accessibility, responsive layouts, or fast pages.

## What comes next?

This post is intentionally simple. Replace it with a real article whenever you are ready, or keep it nearby as a reference for frontmatter, headings, categories, and tags.

```ts
const website = {
  framework: 'Astro',
  mood: 'open notebook',
  languages: ['en', 'ru', 'pl'],
};
```

The useful parts are already wired up: RSS, social previews, structured data, pagination, translated routes, and an automatically generated table of contents.
